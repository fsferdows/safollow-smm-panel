What is SAFollow?
SAFollow is Bangladesh's top-rated SMM reseller panel. We provide high-quality social media engagement services including Facebook Followers, Post Reactions, Instagram Followers, YouTube Views/Subscribers, TikTok Views and Telegram members at the lowest wholesale prices.
How can I deposit money via bKash, Nagad or Rocket?
Log in to your account and click on Add Funds. Select bKash, Nagad, or Rocket. Send money to the provided Personal number, then enter the Transaction ID (TrxID) and the sender number. Once verified, your balance will be updated instantly.
Do you accept Binance Pay / Crypto payments?
Yes! We support Binance Pay (Pay ID) and USDT TRC20/BEP20. You can transfer funds directly and submit the transaction hash or Pay ID to have your account credited in USD automatically.
What should I do if an order is stuck in "Pending"?
Most orders start processing within 0–15 minutes. During peak traffic or server updates, it may take slightly longer. If your order remains pending for more than 2 hours, open a Support Ticket or message us on WhatsApp with your Order ID.
Can I get a custom discount or wholesale rate for high volume?
Yes! If you are a high-volume agency or reseller spending more than $500/month, contact us via WhatsApp for custom pricing discounts and VIP API support.
What is a Mass Order and how do I format it?
Mass Order allows you to place multiple orders simultaneously. Use the format service_id|link|quantity on each new line. For example:
2374|https://facebook.com/post/1|1000
2374|https://facebook.com/post/2|500
